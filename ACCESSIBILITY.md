# Accessibility

Drive HUD is used while driving, so it is built to be read at a glance and used without touching the phone.

## What the app does today
- **Size and position:** the HUD can be made bigger or smaller (60 %–160 %) and moved up or down on the windscreen.
- **Colours:** any colour for each HUD style, a separate colour for the text, and the glow can be turned off for sharper edges.
- **Brightness:** follows the light around the car by itself.
- **Sounds instead of looking:** a beep when over the speed limit, a sound before speed cameras, and a chime at the country's top speed — each can be switched off.
- **Hands-free calls:** the car's or the headset's button answers calls while the HUD rings.
- **No accidental taps:** ending a trip needs a long press.
- **Six languages:** English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian.

## In the future
More accessibility features may come later, such as spoken directions or better support for screen readers. There are no concrete plans yet.
