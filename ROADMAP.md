# Drive HUD · Roadmap

Where Drive HUD is heading, in order. There are no fixed dates and plans may change along the way; ideas from the people who use the app are always welcome in the [issues](https://github.com/V-1per/hud/issues/new/choose).

---

### ✅ Just released · 2.2.8.7 Green Light
A smoother start on any phone.
- A guided setup that gets every feature working in a few steps
- Updates that install from inside the app
- Lighter maps for phones with less memory
- Sounds at speed cameras, with the music lowered for a moment
- The HUD where you see it best, and calendar reminders on it
- WhatsApp calls answered from the car
- Encrypted trip recordings, and the app's permanent signing key

### 🟢 Now in beta · 2.4.2 Open Road
Drive anywhere, maps that come to you, better speed limits and your data safe.
- **Drive without a destination:** tap *Start drive* and the HUD shows your speed, the limit, cameras and bumps ahead and the map around you. Your trip is saved to your timeline when you end it
- Wrong speed limits: after a trip, the app shows the places where the limit seemed wrong, and you can send them so the maps get fixed
- An encrypted copy of your data online, restored on a new phone with a recovery code
- Fewer wrong limits on city avenues
- New countries: France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland and Austria
- Find towns in countries whose map you don't have, and get the maps a long trip crosses
- The app offers the maps of the country you are in
- Speed bump warnings
- Fewer false speeding alarms near towns
- An automatic copy of your data on the phone, to restore after reinstalling
- Send a trip recording to the developer with one tap, no account needed

### 🔵 Coming soon
Better where the road gets hard.
- Better guidance in tunnels and where GPS is weak
- Pothole warnings: the phone feels the bumps on the road, drivers share them, and the HUD warns you before you reach one
- Routes that avoid rough roads for low cars

### 🔵 Next
Your drives, told as a story.
- **Drive HUD Wrapped**: a yearly summary of your trips, your roads and your cars
- More from the garage: each car's history at a glance
- Achievements for your kilometres, trips and countries, that unlock HUD styles and colours
- Community points (for reported bugs and ideas) to exchange for car icons and other extras
- Spoken directions, in your language
- Your own HUD: choose what shows and where

### 🟣 Later
A new map engine.
- Whole countries and long trips across Europe on any phone
- Maps that download by themselves where you drive
- More countries

### ⚪ In the future
- Drive HUD on Google Play
- Friends and leaderboards, with your achievements on an online profile
- Road reports from drivers: accidents, speed checks, traffic and roadworks
- Special places to discover along the way
- iPhone
- A Drive HUD device of its own, made for the car, and one day a projection across the whole windscreen
