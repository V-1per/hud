# Code of conduct

Drive HUD is made by one person with the help of the people who test it. To keep that pleasant for everyone:

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in their second or third language.
- Talk about the app, not about the person.
- No insults, harassment, discrimination, spam or advertising.
- Do not post other people's private information (names, phone numbers, addresses, locations).

Issues or comments that break these rules are edited, hidden or deleted, and repeated cases are blocked from the repository.

To report something that should not be here, use **Report content** in the comment's `…` menu, or open a private report from the [Security tab](https://github.com/V-1per/hud/security).
